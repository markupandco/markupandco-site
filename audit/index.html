Questions

Before you send your site over.

How is this different from a free online tool?

Free tools give you raw scores and a wall of jargon. I run the same underlying checks, then write the plain-English version — what actually matters, what to ignore, and what to fix first — so you're not left guessing what any of it means.

Is my website's data safe?

I only look at what's already publicly visible on your site — the same information anyone gets by visiting it, or that Google itself sees. No login, password, or site access is ever needed for the audit.

What if you don't find anything wrong?

In practice, every real site I've audited has had at least something worth fixing. But if your report doesn't turn up anything useful, you get a full refund — no argument, no fine print.

I'm not technical — will I understand the report?

Yes. That's the whole point. Every finding is explained in plain English first. If you do have a developer, there's a separate technical section formatted for them too.

How long does it take?

2-3 business days from when payment goes through to when your report lands in your inbox.
