How to price handmade items in 2026

The formula most makers learn first is "materials plus labor, times two for wholesale, times four for retail." It's easy to remember and it's wrong often enough to matter: it ignores fees, packaging, overhead and the fact that the buyer's shipping charge gets taxed by Etsy too. The calculator above does cost-plus-margin instead, which is how any business prices: find the true cost of one item, decide what margin you need, and solve for the price with the platform's fees included.

Put together, a normal Etsy sale costs about 9.5% of the order total plus $0.45. An Offsite Ads sale costs about 24.5% plus $0.45. That second number is why a price that looks fine on a spreadsheet can lose money on the sales Etsy sends you from Google Shopping or Pinterest.

Four places craft pricing goes wrong

Not paying yourself. If labor is $0 in your sheet, you're not running a business, you're subsidizing strangers' gifts. Pick a number you'd accept from an employer. $20/hr is a common floor; skilled work is more.

Shipping. Etsy charges its 6.5% and 3% on the shipping you collect, so a $5 shipping charge costs you $0.48 in fees before the label. If you offer "free shipping," the label cost and that fee have to live inside the item price; the calculator handles this if you set shipping charged to $0 and keep the label cost.

Overhead. Booth fees, Etsy Ads, your label printer, the Canva subscription, test batches. Divide a month's worth by the items you sell in a month and put it in the overhead line. For many small shops it's $0.50–$2 per item.

Underpricing to compete. The $9 version of your product on page one is usually someone who hasn't done this math yet. Price from your cost, then make the listing earn it with photos and description.

Which margin?

Direct sales: 35–50%. Etsy: aim for the same, knowing Offsite Ads sales will come in lower. Wholesale: 20–35%, with the check that doubling your wholesale price gives a believable retail price. If it doesn't, either the product isn't a wholesale product or your cost is too high.

Frequently asked questions

How do I price handmade items?

Cost per item (materials + labor + packaging + overhead) divided by (1 − margin), with the platform's fees added. The calculator does the fee math for Etsy, a market and wholesale.

What's the formula for pricing crafts?

The old "×2 wholesale, ×4 retail" rule is a rough proxy for a 50% margin with no fees. Cost-plus-margin with fees is more reliable on Etsy. See the worked example above.

How much does Etsy take from a sale?

About 9.5% of the order total (item + shipping) plus $0.45 on a normal US sale; about 24.5% plus $0.45 if the buyer came through Offsite Ads.

Should I offer free shipping on Etsy?

Etsy favors listings with free shipping over $35 in US search. If you do, fold the label cost into the price and remember the fee is now charged on the whole amount. Set shipping charged to $0 in the calculator to see the effect.

What margin should I aim for?

35–50% direct, 20–35% wholesale. Under 25% is fragile.
