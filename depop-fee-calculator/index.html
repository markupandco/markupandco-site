Depop seller fees in 2026 (US)

Depop dropped its 10% selling fee for US sellers in July 2024 and raised the price of boosting in March 2026. Here is the full picture for a US seller paid through Depop Payments.

Why "no selling fee" still costs 5–10%

The $0.45 is the whole story on cheap items. On a $10 tee with $4.50 shipping, processing is 3.3% of $14.50 plus $0.45 = $0.93. That is 9.3% of the item price. On a $5 item it is over 12%. If most of what you sell is under $15, the flat fee matters more than the percentage, and bundles (one $0.45 for the whole order) are your best friend.

Processing is charged on shipping and tax. You pass shipping through to the buyer, but Depop still takes 3.3% of it. Offering "free shipping" by folding the label into the price doesn't change the fee; it just changes which line it shows up on.

Boosting doubles your fees on small items and more than triples them on big ones. A boosted $75 dress with $6 shipping pays $3.12 processing plus $9.00 boost. Boost only when an item has sat for a while and you'd rather take 12% less than keep it. On a $40 item the boost costs $4.80, so think of it as a $4.80 ad that you only pay if it works.

Worked examples

$40 hoodie, $5 buyer-paid shipping, not boosted, Depop label Processing 3.3% × $45 + $0.45 = $1.94. Payout $45 − $1.94 = $43.06. Label $5, item cost $8, packaging $0.50: net profit $29.56, a 65.7% margin on the $45 the buyer paid.
Same hoodie, boosted Add 12% × $40 = $4.80. Payout $38.26, net profit $24.76. The boost cost you $4.80 of the $29.56.
$12 vintage tee, $4.50 shipping, 8.25% tax, you ship yourself, boosted Tax $0.99. Processing 3.3% × $17.49 + $0.45 = $1.03. Boost 12% × ($12 + $4.50) = $1.98 because no Depop label. Payout $16.50 − $3.01 = $13.49.

How Depop compares

On a $40 item with $5 buyer-paid shipping: Depop keeps $1.94 (4.9% of the item), Mercari charges the buyer its service fee and sellers pay processing, eBay's standard final value fee would be about $6.42 (13.6% of $45 + $0.30), Poshmark takes $8.00 (20%), and Whatnot keeps $4.81 (8% + 2.9% + $0.30; try the Whatnot fee calculator). For clothing under $50, Depop is the cheapest of the big US apps as long as you don't boost.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Depop take from a sale in the US?

No selling fee. You pay 3.3% + $0.45 processing on the total the buyer pays, including shipping and tax. A $40 item with $5 shipping costs $1.94 in fees, so you receive $43.06.

Does Depop charge fees on shipping?

Yes, processing is charged on shipping and tax. The boost fee also includes shipping if you ship on your own instead of using a Depop Shipping label.

What is the boosted listing fee?

12% of the item price (plus shipping without a Depop label) if the boosted listing sells, on top of processing. It went from 8% to 12% for new listings on March 23, 2026.

Do buyers pay a fee?

Yes, Depop adds a marketplace fee at checkout. It isn't taken out of your payout, but it does raise the buyer's total, which is worth remembering when you price.

What if I use PayPal instead of Depop Payments?

US sellers are paid through Depop Payments. PayPal applies to sellers outside the US and UK, who pay the 10% selling fee plus PayPal's own processing fee.

When does Depop pay out?

For tracked US orders Depop releases funds about two working days after delivery; untracked orders after about ten working days. Your bank may add a couple of days.

Do I owe tax on Depop sales?

Depop collects and remits sales tax from buyers. Your selling income is still reportable, and Depop issues a 1099-K once you cross the federal threshold. Keeping fees, item costs and mileage in one place makes that painless; that is what the Reseller Tracker is for. This page is not tax advice.
