What a 3D print really costs

"Filament times three" is the pricing rule you'll see in every print-farm forum, and it's fine until you notice that a 20-hour print of a 300 g part and a 2-hour print of a 300 g part cost you very different amounts of printer. The calculator above costs the five things that actually vary: material (with a failure allowance), printer wear per hour, electricity, your hands-on time, and overhead.

Cost per gram and per hour: the two numbers to know

Material per gram. Spool price ÷ net grams. A $22 1 kg spool is $0.022/g; a $35 spool of PETG-CF is $0.035/g; a $30 1 kg bottle of standard resin is about $0.03/ml. Multiply by what your slicer reports, then add 5–15% for failed prints, purge lines and support material you'll throw away.

Printer per hour. Printer price ÷ expected print hours, plus upkeep, plus electricity. A $600 printer over 4,000 hours is $0.15/hr. Nozzles, build plates, belts and (for resin) FEP film and IPA run $0.03–$0.10/hr depending on the machine. Electricity at 120 W and $0.17/kWh is about $0.02/hr. So roughly $0.20–$0.30 per hour for a typical prosumer FDM machine, more for a $1,500 printer or a high-temp enclosure. Four thousand hours is conservative for a well-maintained machine; if yours lasts 8,000 the wear halves.

Where print-farm pricing goes wrong

Ignoring failures. A 5% failure rate on 20-hour prints is a lot of printer time, not just filament. The calculator applies the allowance to material; if your failures are mostly long prints, bump it up or add the lost hours to upkeep.

Pricing by grams only. Two prints with the same grams can differ 5× in hours (tall thin vase vs. a flat plate). Hours are what limit how many orders you can take, so hours have to be in the price.

Zero labor. Removing supports, sanding, inserting heat-set nuts, bagging and labeling adds up. Eight minutes at $20/hr is $2.67, often more than the filament.

Etsy's fee on shipping. Etsy's 6.5% + 3% applies to the shipping you charge. For heavy prints with $8–$12 shipping that's another $0.80–$1.15 per order the calculator's Etsy price doesn't include; add it to overhead or price shipping with 10% on top.

Policy. Etsy's 2026 rules bar mass-produced items made from designs you didn't create, so models you downloaded from a repository, even with a commercial license, may not be listable. Designs you made yourself are fine. Check the current policy before listing.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to 3D print something?

For FDM, about $0.02–$0.03/g for PLA plus $0.20–$0.30 per printer hour, plus your time. A 100 g, 5-hour print is typically $4–$7 all-in before profit.

How do I calculate filament cost per gram?

Spool price ÷ net grams. $22 ÷ 1,000 = $0.022/g. Multiply by the grams your slicer shows and add a failure allowance.

How much does a 3D printer cost per hour?

Printer price ÷ expected print hours, plus upkeep and electricity. A $600 printer over 4,000 hours with $0.05 upkeep and 120 W draw is about $0.22/hr.

How should I price 3D prints to sell?

Full cost ÷ (1 − margin), with Etsy's fees added if you sell there. The calculator shows direct, Etsy and wholesale prices.

Does this work for resin?

Yes. Switch the material type to resin and enter ml used and bottle price and size. Put FEP film, IPA and gloves in upkeep; washing and curing time in hands-on minutes.
