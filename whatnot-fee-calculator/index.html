Whatnot seller fees in 2026

Whatnot charges US sellers two fees on every paid order, and nothing to list. The calculator above uses these rules.

Three things that surprise new Whatnot sellers

Processing is charged on tax you never receive. Whatnot collects sales tax from the buyer and remits it, but the 2.9% is calculated on the full order including that tax. On a $100 item in a state with 8% tax, that is an extra $0.23 in processing. Small per order; real across a thousand orders.

The $0.30 hurts on $1 starts. A $3 item with no shipping pays $0.24 commission and $0.39 processing, so 21% of the sale is gone before the label. That is why a lot of sellers bundle cheap items into one checkout: the $0.30 is charged per transaction, so a single-checkout lot pays it once.

Your coupons cut your fees, Whatnot's don't. When you issue a seller coupon, commission is figured on the discounted price. When Whatnot issues one (the first-time buyer coupon, for example), fees are figured on the full price and Whatnot eats the discount.

Worked examples

$50 fashion item, $6 buyer-paid shipping, no tax Commission 8% × $50 = $4.00. Processing 2.9% × $56 + $0.30 = $1.92. Payout $56 − $5.92 = $50.08. If the label cost $5.50 and you paid $12 for the item, net profit is $32.58.
$25 sports card, $4.50 shipping, 8.25% tax Tax = $2.06. Commission $2.00. Processing 2.9% × ($25 + $4.50 + $2.06) + $0.30 = $1.22. Payout $29.50 − $3.22 = $26.28.
$2,000 graded card (high-value promo) Commission 8% on the first $1,500 = $120; 0% on the remaining $500. Processing 2.9% × $2,000 + $0.30 = $58.30. Payout $1,821.70 before shipping.

How Whatnot compares

On a $50 item with no shipping, Whatnot keeps $5.75 (11.5%). eBay's standard final value fee in most categories is 13.6% + $0.30 on the total including shipping, Poshmark takes a flat 20% on sales over $15, Mercari charges buyers a service fee but sellers pay payment processing, and Depop US has no selling fee but 3.3% + $0.45 processing (try the Depop fee calculator). Whatnot is cheaper than Poshmark and eBay on most items; the trade is that you are live on camera to earn it.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Whatnot take from a sale?

8% commission on the item price (4% for Coins & Money) plus 2.9% + $0.30 processing on the whole order. On a $50 item with no shipping, that is $5.75, so you receive $44.25.

Does Whatnot charge fees on shipping?

Commission, no. Processing, yes: the 2.9% + $0.30 is calculated on the order total, which includes buyer-paid shipping and sales tax.

Are there fees on giveaways?

No. The buyer pays nothing, so there is no commission or processing. You cover the label.

Is there a listing fee or a monthly subscription?

No. Creating, storing and managing listings is free, and there is no seller subscription.

What does the 0% above $1,500 mean?

For a limited time, in Comics, TCG, Sports Singles, Toys & Hobbies and Coins & Money, only the first $1,500 of an item's price is charged commission. Processing still applies to the full order.

When does Whatnot pay out?

Whatnot releases funds after the order is confirmed shipped and the payment clears; timing depends on your payout method and account history. Check Seller Hub for your schedule.

Do I owe tax on Whatnot sales?

Whatnot collects and remits sales tax for buyers. Your income from selling is still reportable, and Whatnot issues a 1099-K once you cross the federal threshold.
